Types of Relationships:
a)One to One
b)One to Many
c)Many to Many

a)One to One
let suppose,we have two tables,"employees" and "employee details",logically har individual employee ki personal detail srf 1 bar he "employee details" table mei aiy gi,isy 1 to 1 relationship kehty ha.See One to One image

b)One to many
let suppose,we have two tables,"employees" and "employee tasks",har employee ka 1 task b hu skta ha aur more then 1 task b hu skty ha,is relationship ko one to many kehty ha.See One to Many image

c)Many to Many
let suppose,we have two tables,"authors" and "books",one book can be written by multiple authors and each author can write multiple books,this is called many to many relationship.See image

How to create foreign key?
let suppose,we have two tables, customers & orders
create table customers(id Serial Primary key,name varchar(100) not null);
create table orders(id Serial Primary key,order_bill decimal(10,2) not null,cust_id int not null,foreign key(cust_id) references customers(id));
